    Privacy Policy

    Effective: 1 January 2026 · Entity: CKA BuildStruct Private Limited

    CKA BuildStruct may collect submitted contact details, BOQs, drawings, delivery locations and supplier documents for review and service-related communication.

    What we collect

    • Name, phone, WhatsApp number, email and city submitted through forms.
    • Project documents (BOQs, drawings, references) you upload for quotation preparation.
    • Delivery locations and site details required to coordinate dispatches.

    How we use it

    • To prepare quotations, comparison sheets and supplier tenders on your request.
    • To communicate order status, delivery coordination and design consultations.
    • To verify suppliers and maintain reliability records.

    Sharing

    Project requirements are shared with screened suppliers only after your approval. We do not sell personal data. Documents are retained for active project duration plus 24 months.

    Your choices

    Email info@ckabuildstruct.com to access, correct or delete your inquiry records.

    Terms & Conditions

    Effective: 1 January 2026

    These are the basic conditions for using CKA BuildStruct services, including the material marketplace, live bidding, design studio and procurement coordination.

    Rates & quotations

    • Marketplace prices are indicative market rates, verified daily, and may change with supplier markets.
    • A quotation becomes binding only when confirmed in writing with quantity, delivery point and validity window.

    Orders & payments

    • Payments are accepted via bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa and COD (limits apply).
    • Credit terms require account verification and a signed credit application.

    Delivery & quality

    • Delivery timelines are committed per quotation; delays beyond 48 hours are proactively communicated.
    • Quality claims must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.

    Platform use

    Submitted BOQs and drawings are used solely for service delivery. Misuse of the tender board, false registrations or rate manipulation leads to account suspension.
